# About Cedar Hill

Cedar Hill Retreat Center is owned and operated as a Massachusetts non-profit corporation under the leadership of our Board of Directors.

## Contact Information

We don't have an onsite office, so the best way to reach us is by e-mail **cedarhillretreat@gmail.com** or by phone **781-217-4540**. We will be happy to provide information and answer all your questions.

## Who We Serve

Many diverse groups use our facilities for their classes, conferences and retreat needs:

* Parish staff and church groups
* Community groups and social service agencies  
* Yoga groups and wellness practitioners
* Youth groups and school faculty
* Small non-profit organizations
* Family retreat groups

## Custom Solutions

Since your needs are unique, please e-mail us, and we will be glad to gather the details of your stay and give you a quote based on your needs.
